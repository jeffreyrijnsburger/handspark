HandSpark 0.4.0 gives thumbs up more character and refreshes the app icon.

- A larger golden thumb pops into a mint-colored badge with spring motion, a small nod and colorful round confetti.
- The gesture stays readable at 360p, and its camera placement leaves room for the animation.
- The desktop/taskbar icon is now a clear raised hand with simple motion lines. The four-point sparkle is gone.
- The README includes a preview made from the actual encoded effect.

Camera output remains configurable at 360p, 540p or 720p / 30 fps. Preview settings are independent and leave the meeting-camera device free for video apps. Existing language preferences are preserved. Autostart is disabled by default.

The `.deb` targets Ubuntu 24.04 amd64 / Python 3.12 / X11 and bundles inference dependencies, models and effects. OBS 30.2+, Qt/FFmpeg and a working V4L2 loopback driver remain system dependencies. See README.md for installation.
